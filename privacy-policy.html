Privacy Policy for Pop Orb - Ball Merge

Effective date: 6 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sooren Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Pop Orb - Ball Merge ("the Game") on Android. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, skins, settings and a saved game in progress) is stored on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google account (cloud save) so you do not lose it after reinstalling. Items bought with real money (such as "No Ads") are restored from Google Play automatically.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses services from Google that may automatically collect some information from your device. These services act under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) - may collect the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device model, operating system and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. How Google uses data in ads
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) - may collect crash reports, diagnostic data, device information, app usage events and installation identifiers to help us fix bugs and improve the Game. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Play Games Services (cloud save and leaderboards) - if you sign in with Play Games, your game progress is saved to your Google account so it can be restored after reinstalling or on a new phone. This uses your Play Games player ID, gamer name (nickname) and profile picture. The leaderboards show your gamer name, profile picture and free-mode scores to other players. You can play without signing in. Play Games privacy information
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) - payments are processed by Google Play. We receive only purchase confirmations; we never see your card or payment details. Google Payments privacy notice

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How the information is used

  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To find and fix crashes and improve game balance
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

5. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: on Android open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required by law, the Game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice later in the Game's settings.
  • Remove ads: you can buy the "No Ads" option in the Game's shop.
  • Deletion: uninstall the Game to delete all data stored on your device. To ask about data held by the services above, contact us (section 10).

How to request deletion of your data (Pop Orb - Ball Merge, Sooren Games)

  1. Game progress on your device: open Android Settings > Apps > Pop Orb > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the Game. This deletes the progress stored on the phone. Your cloud save stays in your Google account, so you can get it back later.
  2. Cloud save: open the Google Play Games app > Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data (choose Pop Orb to delete only this game's data).
  3. Data held by our services (crash reports, analytics, ad data): send an email to soorengames.official@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data - Pop Orb".
  4. We reply within 30 days and delete the crash and analytics data linked to your device.

What is deleted: crash reports, diagnostic data and analytics events linked to your device.
What may be kept: purchase records, which Google Play keeps as required by tax and payment laws, and ad data held by Google AdMob under Google's own policy (you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings at any time).
Retention: even without a request, crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days and analytics data linked to your device after 2 months.

6. Data security and retention

Data sent by the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Crash and analytics data is kept only as long as needed to fix problems and improve the Game, under the retention rules of those services.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

Use of the Game is also governed by our Terms of Service. We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Pop Orb saves your game progress (levels, coins, gems, items and settings) to your Google account using Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can continue on another device. This data is stored by Google under your account and is used only to restore your progress. You can delete it at any time in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account & data) or by contacting soorengames.official@gmail.com.
